Safety

Safety is part of every wall we build.

We work to OSHA standards on every Dallas Fort Worth jobsite, from the first scaffold frame to the final coat.

Safety on site

Safe work is
good work.

Stucco happens on scaffolds, around mixers and next to other trades. We plan every job so the people on it, and the people around it, stay safe.

I OSHA 1926.451

Scaffolds

Set on solid footing, fully planked and braced, and checked before each shift the way OSHA requires.

II OSHA 1926.95

Protection

Hard hats, high-visibility vests, eye protection and gloves on every crew member, plus fall protection wherever the work is high.

III OSHA 1926.25

Clean sites

Materials staged out of walkways, dust kept down when we mix and cut, and a clean site at the end of every day.

Seen from the street

Look for the
pink scaffolds.

Our pink scaffolds are hard to miss. Builders, inspectors and neighbors always know where our crew is working, and every frame goes up to the same standard.

OSHA certified crews

Every crew on an LVA site works to the same OSHA standard. Before work starts, the crew walks the site, checks the scaffolds and talks through the day. If something is not safe, the work stops until it is.
